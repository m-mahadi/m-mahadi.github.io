I am starting before I am ready

I am starting this site before it feels polished because waiting for the perfect version is another way of not starting.

I want to write about AI, building software, reading, music, making videos, and the strange practical work of becoming a founder. Some of it will be clear. Some of it will be early. That is the point.

My current public project is Typi, a small typewriter simulator and writing app for people who want a quiet place to write their own words into Markdown.
